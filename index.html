Research

Deep learning for medical imaging

My research covers MSK, breast, neuro and thoracic imaging, from fracture detection to breast cancer risk prediction. I've also written educational reviews to help radiologists judge AI studies critically.

MSK · Fracture detection

Automated femoral neck fracture detection & classification

Deep-learning detection and Garden classification of hip fractures on radiographs, with data augmentation for small medical datasets. Covered by Health Imaging.

J Digit Imaging, 2020 · doi

Pediatric · Bone age

MABAL: machine-assisted bone age labeling

A deep-learning architecture for automated bone-age assessment from hand radiographs, later extended to trauma radiographs in Radiology: AI.

J Digit Imaging, 2018 · doi

Breast · Risk & triage

CNN-based breast cancer risk and DCIS selection

Mammographic risk models and an algorithm distinguishing atypical ductal hyperplasia from DCIS, funded by an NIH StARR R38 grant.

Acad Radiol, 2020 · doi

Education · Review

Clinical AI applications: musculoskeletal

A review of where MSK AI stands clinically, plus a Clinical Imaging series on overfitting and CNN architecture for non-specialists.

Radiol Clin North Am, 2021 · doi
