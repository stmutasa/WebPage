About

Radiologist, engineer, builder.

I’m a board-certified musculoskeletal radiologist based in New York. I trained in diagnostic radiology at NewYork-Presbyterian/Columbia, where I served two years as chief resident, and completed my musculoskeletal fellowship at Thomas Jefferson University Hospital.

I came to medicine from engineering. Before medical school I was a drilling and completions engineer at Halliburton, and that habit of building things stayed with me. As a resident I worked on more than 60 AI research projects and wrote the code for most of them myself. That work has led to 38 peer-reviewed publications, an NIH-funded resident research grant, and invited faculty roles teaching AI to radiologists at RSNA.

Today I read musculoskeletal and general radiology for hospitals and teleradiology groups through my company, Ugen Imaging. I’m most interested in subspecialty-quality reads delivered anywhere, and in clinical AI that actually holds up in practice.

Apps

I build software for problems in my own life: health, relationships, money and time. I design each app and build it with AI coding agents. Each is a full-stack app that is deployed and in use, and most put AI at the core rather than bolting it on. All are private, single-user tools.

  • Housecall

    Your doctor in the family.

    Pulls Whoop, Garmin, Withings and Omron data into one dashboard and writes a goals-first health brief every morning, delivered as a push notification. Meals are logged by photo or text and analyzed by AI.

    Next.js · Prisma · Android (Capacitor) · Claude Vision

  • Juli-a

    A health companion built around the cycle.

    Puts the menstrual cycle at the center of training, nutrition, fasting, supplements and mood, with a weekly AI plan that explains the why behind every recommendation. It replaced a spreadsheet that had been kept since 2018.

    Expo / React Native · iOS TestFlight · Fastify · Postgres

  • Kindred

    Keep the people you meet.

    A personal relationship CRM. Capture who you met and what you talked about, get nudged to follow up at the right cadence, and get AI-drafted messages and pre-meeting briefs. Success is measured by turning a first meeting into a second.

    Next.js · Google sign-in & sync · Web push

  • DoneX

    An AI task companion.

    Tasks, notes and a voice assistant, plus a morning briefing and a one-tap day plan. A unified inbox triages Gmail and text messages into to-dos. It’s self-hosted as a single container.

    Next.js · SQLite · Installable PWA

  • PortViz

    Portfolio visualization.

    A treemap of public holdings colored by return, a pre-IPO register marked to secondary-market prices, and a 1–10 margin-risk score. The arithmetic is deterministic; AI is used only for judgment and weekly price research.

    Python / FastAPI · dependency-free JS · PWA

  • The Iron Condor

    An options trading desk.

    Live charts and technicals for a watchlist, plus a daily briefing in which Claude and ChatGPT each write an independent take, review the other’s, and an arbiter model reconciles them into one read.

    Next.js · multi-model AI pipeline · Android PWA

Outside medicine

In winter I’m on the mountain whenever the schedule allows. On clear nights I’m usually looking up: astronomy is a long-standing interest, and it has something in common with radiology. Both are about finding a faint signal in a great deal of noise.

The rest of the time I’m a committed gastronome. New York makes that an easy habit, and a great meal is my favorite way to end a long week of reading.
